How applied AI systems actually work

Making applied AI systems easier to understand, inspect and build.

Everyone says AI. Pass it through a prism and it comes apart into the parts that do the work, with a person at every one. Pick a colour to read about it.

Start with the map →Flip through a storyboard →

A beam labelled AI enters a glass prism and leaves as seven bands: the model, instructions, context, tools, the harness, permissions and evaluation. A small figure marked a human, at every stage, runs through every band: a person chooses the model, writes the instructions, decides what it sees and what it can reach, sets when the loop stops, decides what needs approval and defines what good means. Scrolling passes the bands through a lens and a second prism, which gather them back into one beam: a system you understand.
