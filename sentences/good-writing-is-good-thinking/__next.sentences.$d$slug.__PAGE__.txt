<p>Clear writing is a service to your reader. But more than that, it is a service to yourself. It is a tool for clarifying your own understanding. If you cannot explain an idea simply, you do not understand it well enough.</p>
<p>This is why we must write. Not just to communicate, but to think. The page is a mirror for our minds.</p>
<p><strong>What this changes in practice:</strong> If you feel stuck on a problem, try to explain it in writing. The act of writing will often reveal the solution.</p>
