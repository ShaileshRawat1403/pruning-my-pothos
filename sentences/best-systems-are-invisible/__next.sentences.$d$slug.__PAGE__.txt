<p>This invisibility is a sign of success. It means the system has become so intuitive, so reliable, that it requires no conscious effort to use. The friction is gone. The work flows through it.</p>
<p>The goal of a designer or a builder should be to create this kind of seamless experience. To build systems that are so effective they disappear, leaving only the task at hand.</p>
<p><strong>What this changes in practice:</strong> Design for the background, not the foreground. Aim to reduce cognitive load until your system feels like an extension of the user&#39;s own mind.</p>
