<p>Every act of discipline is an act of self-respect. It is the decision to trade short-term comfort for long-term growth. It is the recognition that your goals are more important than your moods. This builds a foundation of trust within yourself.</p>
<p>This is not about perfection. It is about the choice to return to the path when you stray. It is the gentle, firm correction that keeps you aligned with your values.</p>
<p><strong>What this changes in practice:</strong> Frame acts of discipline not as chores, but as investments in the person you want to be.</p>
