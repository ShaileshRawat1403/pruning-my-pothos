<p>An AI can mimic the pattern of an expert answer. It can use the jargon, the structure, and the tone. But it cannot weigh the trade-offs that are not written down. It cannot feel the weight of a decision.</p>
<p>Expertise is not about knowing the answer. It is about knowing which questions matter in a specific moment. It is a filter, not a generator.</p>
<p><strong>What this changes in practice:</strong> Use AI to generate options, but keep the expert in the loop to select the truth.</p>
