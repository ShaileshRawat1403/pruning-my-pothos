<p>This transaction is risky. Meaning relies on shared context, history, and judgment—things a model does not have. It has only patterns. It can mimic the sound of understanding without doing the work of comprehension.</p>
<p>The gap between what we say and what the machine computes is where systems fail. We are outsourcing the labor of interpretation to a statistical engine that has never experienced the world it describes.</p>
<p><strong>What this changes in practice:</strong> Define your terms explicitly in the prompt, or accept that the machine will define them for you.</p>
