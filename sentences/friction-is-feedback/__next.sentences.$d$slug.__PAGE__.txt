<p>But friction is not just a problem. It is also a form of feedback. It is a signal that the system is resisting the change we are trying to make. It is a sign that there is a conflict between our intention and the reality of the situation.</p>
<p>To treat friction as feedback is to shift our mindset from one of annoyance to one of curiosity. Instead of asking &quot;How can I get rid of this friction?&quot; we ask &quot;What is this friction telling me?&quot;</p>
<p><strong>What this changes in practice:</strong> When you encounter friction, don&#39;t just push through it. Pause and listen. What is the source of the resistance? What is the system trying to tell you?</p>
