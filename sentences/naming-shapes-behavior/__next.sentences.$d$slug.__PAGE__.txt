<p>When we name something, we are making a decision about what matters. We are drawing a boundary around a concept and giving it a distinct identity. This act of naming is an act of power. It is the power to define, to include, and to exclude.</p>
<p>Choose your names with care. A good name can clarify a difficult idea. A bad name can create confusion and mistrust. A name is a small thing, but it has the power to shape behavior on a large scale.</p>
<p><strong>What this changes in practice:</strong> When you are trying to change a behavior, start by changing the name. A new name can create a new way of seeing.</p>
