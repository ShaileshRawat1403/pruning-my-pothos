17:T7eb,<p class="lead">I used to debug only when things broke. Now I review behavior before trust drifts.</p>

<p>Every week, I pick one workflow and replay it from logs, not memory. I check what the model proposed, what the runtime allowed, and what evidence marked completion. The goal is simple: find one silent assumption before it becomes production folklore.</p>
<p>This ritual is less glamorous than shipping features, but it is where quality compounds. <span class="highlight">Systems decay in small increments</span>, and a weekly reset catches those increments while they are still cheap to fix.</p>
<p>I also write one line after each review: &quot;what signal would have told me earlier?&quot; That question keeps my attention on instrumentation, not blame. Over time, it has changed how I design flows. I now ask for observability at design time instead of bolting it on after incidents.</p>
