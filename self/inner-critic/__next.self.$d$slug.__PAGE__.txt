2:T5b2,<p class="lead">The inner critic is a misdirected protector.</p>

<p>It tries to keep you safe by keeping you small. You do not need to silence it; you need to <span class="highlight">reassign its job</span>.</p>
<p>Ask it for specifics. What exactly is wrong? What small change would help? Vague fear becomes a clear task when you make it concrete.</p>
