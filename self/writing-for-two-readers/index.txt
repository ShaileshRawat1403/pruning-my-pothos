17:T7e1,<p class="lead">I now assume every page has two readers: a person and a retrieval engine.</p>

<p>For a long time, I treated these as opposing audiences. Human writing felt nuanced, while search writing felt mechanical. That split was false. What both readers want is <span class="highlight">clarity under constraint</span>.</p>
<p>When I name ideas consistently, structure arguments cleanly, and keep claims specific, humans read faster and machines retrieve better. The page does not lose voice. It gains precision.</p>
<p>The shift is not &quot;write for algorithms.&quot; The shift is to reduce ambiguity where ambiguity adds no value. I still write in my tone, but I am more intentional about how each section can stand as evidence.</p>
